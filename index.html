Merge Puzzle | Unity 2D

ClawsTruction

Developed as a prototype for the UTN Game Jam 2026 by a 4-person team. ClawsTruction is a precision-based merge puzzle built in Unity, where players construct specific target figures by dropping objects via a moving claw. As a QA Tester and Co-Developer, I ensured the stability of core mechanics and the reliability of complex custom systems across the game's levels.

Key Systems & QA Focus

  • Spawn Logic: Validation of dynamic prioritization queues for object generation across 3 distinct levels.
  • Physics Verification: Testing of collision boundaries, drop timing ranges, and trajectory limits within the Unity environment.
  • Algorithm Testing: Strict verification of the 100-point matching metric, accounting for proximity and rotation up to 45 degrees.
  • Progression Balancing: Evaluation of the 3-star reward system thresholds (40%, 65%, 85%) for difficulty tuning.
